# Case 01 · Lead → Sale

**How a car dealership stopped losing 1 in 4 interested customers**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Redwood Auto Group (fictional) · Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas · 6 salespeople · ~500 leads per month |
| **Problem** | *"We get lots of interested customers, but we sell very few cars."* |
| **My role** | Process diagnosis, workflow redesign, implementation and measurement |
| **Tools** | Mermaid (process maps) · HTML/JS (form) · Google Apps Script + Sheets (automation) · Chart.js (dashboard) |

## Executive summary

**Diagnosis.** Leads arrived through 7 channels into 5 different places, with no record and no owner. **24%** never got a reply, and the median time to first contact was **17.7 hours**. The data showed that a lead contacted within 1 hour converts **2.2× more** than one contacted after 24 hours.

**Proposal.** A workflow with one front door (form + sheet), automatic and even assignment, an instant notice to the salesperson, a reminder at 1 hour and escalation to the manager at 4 hours. All built on Google Sheets and Gmail, with no new licenses.

**Pilot result (3 months):**

| KPI | Before | Pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Leads contacted | 75.6% | **90.2%** |
| Median time to first contact | 17.7 h | **~1 h** |
| Lead → sale conversion | 6.8% | **10.7%** |
| Sales per month | 34 | **55** |

**Estimated value (base scenario):** ~**$27K in additional gross profit per month (~$327K per year)**, for ~40 hours of implementation and no new software licenses.
