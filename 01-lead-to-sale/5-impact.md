# 5 · Impact: pilot results and estimated value

> ⚠️ The data is **synthetic**, built to illustrate the method. In a real engagement, this same template is filled with the company's own data.

## 5.1 Results: current process vs. pilot

| KPI | Current process<br/>(Jan–Jun) | Pilot<br/>(Jul–Sep) | Target | Met? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leads per month | 504 | 516 | — | — |
| % of leads contacted | 75.6% | **90.2%** | ≥ 90% | ✅ |
| Median time to first contact | 17.7 h | **~1 h** | ≤ 1 h | ✅ (borderline) |
| % of contacts after 24 h | 40.3% | **7.6%** | ≤ 10% | ✅ |
| Uncontacted leads per month | 123 | **51** | — | ▼ 59% |
| Lead → sale conversion | 6.8% | **10.7%** | ≥ 9% | ✅ |
| Sales per month | 34.2 | **55.0** | — | ▲ 61% |

## 5.2 Estimated economic value

**Formula:** `leads per month × conversion uplift × gross profit per vehicle`

```
504 leads × (10.65% − 6.79%) × $2,980  ≈  19.5 sales  ≈  $58.0K additional gross profit per month
```

The full pilot result is **not attributed to the new workflow alone**. Seasonality may play a part, teams try harder when they know they're being measured (the pilot effect), and the market can shift. That's why three scenarios are presented:

| Scenario | % of uplift attributed to the workflow | Extra sales per month | Extra gross profit per month | Extra gross profit per year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 25% | ~4.9 | ~$14.5K | ~$174K |
| **Base** | **50%** | **~9.7** | **~$29.0K** | **~$348K** |
| Optimistic | 100% | ~19.5 | ~$58.0K | ~$696K |

## 5.3 Cost of the solution

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Software | $0 extra: Google Sheets + Gmail for the pilot, then the CRM the dealership already pays for |
| Implementation | ~40 hours of analysis and setup |
| Operation | ~5 min per day for the sales manager to review alerts + 30 sec per manually logged lead |

**Even in the conservative scenario, the investment pays for itself in the first month.**

## 5.4 Other benefits (not monetized)

- **Visibility:** the sales manager knows every day which leads are pending and who owns them.
- **Fairness:** the workload is even across salespeople, with no fighting over "who gets the lead".
- **A base to keep improving:** with loss reasons recorded, the next analysis can tackle price, stock or financing.

## 5.5 Recommended next steps

1. Move the workflow rules into the dealership's CRM and connect a business texting platform, so every lead is logged automatically.
2. Assign leads based on each salesperson's current workload instead of plain round-robin.
3. Analyze loss reasons: which quotes don't close, and why.
