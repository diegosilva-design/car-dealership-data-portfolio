Synthetic data generated for this portfolio. The company is fictional.

Key metrics i Each card shows the selected month, compared with:
LM · last month
L12M · monthly average of the 12 months before the selected month
YoY · the same month last year
Target · the monthly goal (set per channel, so it is hidden when a salesperson is selected)
Rates are compared in percentage points (pp); counts and money in %. ▲/▼ show direction; green is better, red is worse.

Conversion funnel i How many of the month's leads reached each stage of the sale: first contact, test drive, price quote and sale. The width of each band is the share of leads that got that far, and the small text under each stage shows how many moved on from the previous one. On the right, L12M is the same share averaged over the last 12 months. Hover a band for details.

Leads per month i New leads received each month this year (bars), against the monthly target (dark line) and the same month last year (gray line). The selected month is highlighted. Use the channel menu on this card to look at one lead source at a time.

Sales per month i Cars sold each month from that month's leads (bars), against the monthly sales target (dark line) and the same month last year (gray line). Follows the channel and salesperson filters at the top. Targets are hidden when a salesperson is selected.

Speed to lead i Of the leads that were contacted, the share that ended in a sale, grouped by how long the first contact took. Uses all months before the pilot (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026) and excludes walk-ins, who are attended on the spot. This is the evidence behind the 1-hour response target.

Lead → sale conversion by hours to first contact · Jan 2025 – Jun 2026

Contact rate by channel i Share of leads that received at least one reply from the sales team, by the channel the lead came from. The selected month (blue) is compared with the L12M average (gray). Channels are sorted from least to most attended over the last 12 months.

Channel scorecard i Leads and sales for the selected month by channel, against each channel's monthly target. ✓ target met · ● within 10% of target · ✗ more than 10% below target. Conv. vs L12M is the change in conversion rate, in percentage points, against the last-12-month average.

Sales team i Each salesperson's results for the selected month. Share of leads shows how evenly leads were distributed (the pilot assigns them in rotation). Contact rates below 70% are flagged with ⚠. Conversion is compared with the salesperson's own L12M average.
